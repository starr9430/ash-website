# ASH Visual System v0.1

> Working visual direction for the ASH overseas B2B website.
>
> Core statement: **米白纸张作为舞台，深色形成结构，叶片 / 水流成为品牌符号，机械金属成为产品核心，所有东西之间形成一种非常克制的自然 × 工业关系。**

## 1. Brand idea

ASH should feel like a **quiet industrial brand**, not a traditional automotive-parts catalogue.

The visual tension is:

- **Nature** gives the brand origin, softness and memory.
- **Industry** gives the brand precision, weight and credibility.
- **Whitespace** gives both sides enough room to breathe.

Do not turn the website into a literal "nature-themed automotive website".

The natural language must stay abstract and restrained. The industrial language must stay tactile and physical.

---

## 2. Color system

### Stage / Paper

The page background is a warm off-white, closer to paper than a sterile web white.

- Paper: `#EE ECE6` → implemented as `#eeece6`
- Paper light: `#F8F7F3`
- Paper dark: `#E3E0D8`

Use paper tones for the majority of page area.

### Structure / Ink

Dark ink is the primary structural color.

- Ink: `#1D201D`
- Ink soft: `#5D615C`

Use deep ink for:

- navigation emphasis
- major headings
- strong structural blocks
- CTA backgrounds
- large typography

Avoid pure black.

### Metal

Metal is a material language, not a decorative accent.

- Metal mid: `#969B95`
- Metal light: `#D1D1CA`

Metal should appear through:

- product photography
- gradients
- reflective surfaces
- thin mechanical lines
- subtle texture

Avoid chrome effects and excessive glossy 3D styling.

### Brand natural accent

ASH uses a restrained grey-green / moss accent.

- Moss: `#657066`
- Moss light: `#AAB5AA`

The accent should be sparse and recognizable.

Rule of thumb:

> **Moss identifies. Ink structures. Paper carries. Metal describes.**

---

## 3. Typography

Typography should feel editorial and engineered at the same time.

### Display

Large headlines are:

- large
- quiet
- low-weight
- tightly tracked
- short in wording

Preferred construction:

`Statement.`
`Secondary statement.`

Use italics sparingly through the ASH accent color rather than decorative type effects.

### Body

Body copy should be neutral and highly readable.

- 15–17px on desktop
- 12–14px for compact metadata
- generous line height
- restrained color contrast

### Micro type

Use uppercase micro-labels for navigation, section numbers and metadata.

Characteristics:

- 9–11px
- 0.14–0.20em letter spacing
- medium / bold weight
- muted opacity

These create the "industrial specification" layer.

---

## 4. Spatial system

Whitespace is one of ASH's primary brand assets.

Do not fill empty space simply because it is available.

### Container

Maximum content width:

`1400px`

Desktop page edges:

`24–48px`

Internal section padding:

`7vw` as a starting point.

### Rhythm

Use a small number of spacing families rather than arbitrary values:

- XS: 8
- S: 16
- M: 24
- L: 40
- XL: 64
- 2XL: 96
- 3XL: 150+

Large vertical transitions should feel intentional.

---

## 5. Grid

The page should feel editorial rather than card-based.

Preferred structures:

- 2-column asymmetric hero
- wide text + narrow supporting copy
- numbered horizontal lists
- full-width dark structural blocks
- large isolated product compositions

Avoid default web patterns such as:

- equal 3-column cards everywhere
- boxed content modules
- excessive rounded cards
- dashboard-like information density

---

## 6. Natural language

### Leaf

The leaf is not an illustration pasted onto the page.

Its role is:

- origin
- growth
- branching
- organic geometry

Use leaf forms as:

- faint outlines
- cropped silhouettes
- veins / branching structures
- subtle watermark-like forms

Do not use bright green leaves, stock botanical photos, or literal eco-brand imagery.

### Water

Water represents movement, continuity and source.

Use:

- flowing curves
- contour lines
- soft reflections
- layered translucent shapes
- long horizontal / diagonal movement

Water should usually be perceived before it is understood.

---

## 7. Industrial language

Mechanical components are the factual anchor of the brand.

The visual treatment should favor:

- cast metal
- steel
- friction material
- machined edges
- grooves
- holes
- concentric structures
- measured geometry

Product imagery should feel physical.

Preferred lighting:

- directional
- controlled
- soft enough to preserve shape
- enough shadow to reveal depth

Avoid:

- floating product cutouts on generic blue backgrounds
- fake lens flares
- excessive reflections
- cheap 3D renders that look disconnected from a real product

---

## 8. Nature × Industrial composition

The core composition rule is **contrast without conflict**.

Good:

> pale paper + dark structure + quiet moss + real metal

Good:

> a mechanical disc partially intersected by a water contour

Good:

> a leaf vein becoming a technical line

Bad:

> green leaves surrounding a clutch

Bad:

> industrial product with random nature graphics

Bad:

> adding green to every component

The two worlds should feel as though they belong to the same material system.

---

## 9. Imagery hierarchy

The website should have four image roles.

### A. Brand atmosphere

Very wide, quiet, abstract imagery.

Examples:

- paper texture
- water surface
- shadow
- branch structure
- close material studies

### B. Product hero

A single hero product or component with strong physical presence.

Composition should leave intentional negative space for copy.

### C. Technical detail

Close crops of:

- friction surface
- splines
- spring structure
- machining
- material transitions

### D. Application context

Only where it adds useful information.

Examples:

- vehicle silhouettes
- workshop context
- commercial vehicles

Do not let application photography overwhelm the brand system.

---

## 10. Motion

Animation should be quiet.

Preferred:

- slow fade
- subtle translation
- very small scale changes
- line drawing
- parallax measured in a few pixels

Avoid:

- aggressive scroll effects
- bouncing UI
- excessive hover animation
- cinematic effects on every section

A good ASH page should still feel composed when all animation is removed.

---

## 11. UI components

Buttons should be sparse.

Primary CTA:

- dark ink background
- paper text
- compact rectangular form
- no pill shape

Secondary actions:

- text links
- thin underline
- directional arrow

Cards should be used only when grouping real information requires them.

---

## 12. Product section rule

The product catalogue is not the first thing the visitor should see.

The narrative order should generally be:

1. Brand / promise
2. Approach
3. Coverage
4. Capability
5. Products / applications
6. Contact

The website should sell **confidence in sourcing**, not simply display SKUs.

---

## 13. What ASH must never become

Never drift toward:

- generic Chinese auto-parts export website
- green "eco" brand
- premium luxury car aesthetic
- overdesigned tech startup
- catalogue grid
- stock-photo corporate website
- fake OEM claims
- exaggerated quality claims

---

## 14. Current implementation status

v0.1 currently contains:

- paper / ink / moss / metal color tokens
- responsive editorial grid
- oversized display typography
- restrained navigation
- abstract clutch-inspired hero graphic
- approach / coverage / capability / contact sections

v0.2 should focus on turning the abstract visual direction into actual reusable assets:

- ASH leaf / water motif
- real clutch product photography
- metal material studies
- background textures
- hero composition
- reusable product image treatment

Only after those assets exist should the final Hero and major section layouts be refined.
