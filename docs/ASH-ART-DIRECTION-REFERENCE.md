# ASH Art Direction Reference

## Reference image

The current master visual reference is the supplied ASH homepage Art Direction image.

Its role is not to be copied pixel-for-pixel. It defines the intended visual relationships, hierarchy, material language and composition grammar for the real website.

## Core composition

### 1. Paper stage

The page is built on a warm, matte, ivory paper-like field.

The background should feel tactile and softly lit rather than flat white.

### 2. Editorial navigation

The header behaves like a restrained editorial masthead:

- warm light background
- custom ASH mark at left
- compact uppercase navigation
- contact / language utility information at right
- thin rules
- generous horizontal breathing room

### 3. Hero architecture

The hero is a two-sided composition:

**Left**
- ASH brand mark / wordmark
- product category
- short brand statement
- compact CTA

**Right**
- real clutch assembly
- leaf / water ribbons
- soft directional light
- large negative space around the product

The natural forms are not a background decoration. They physically connect brand copy and product.

### 4. Nature language

The supplied reference establishes three recurring natural forms:

- elongated leaf silhouette
- leaf vein / branching line
- flowing water ribbon

The forms should use muted natural tones and translucent layers.

They should feel almost like a physical graphic material placed over paper.

Avoid literal eco imagery.

### 5. Industrial language

The product is the strongest physical object in the composition.

Preferred treatment:

- real clutch assembly photography
- visible machined metal
- controlled highlights
- dark friction material
- believable shadows
- slight overlap / depth between components

The product should not look like a generic cutout pasted over a webpage.

### 6. Typography system

The reference uses two complementary type personalities.

**Sans-serif**
- navigation
- micro labels
- specifications
- utility information
- body copy

**Editorial serif**
- selected section headlines
- product storytelling
- larger explanatory statements

The serif should be used sparingly. The overall site remains modern and restrained.

### 7. Accent color

The strongest brand color is a deep blue-green / petrol tone.

It appears in:

- ASH logo details
- Hero ribbon
- primary CTA
- footer
- selected typography accents

It must remain controlled rather than becoming a generic website theme color.

### 8. Section transition

The reference uses a narrow information rail immediately after the Hero.

This rail contains four compact value propositions with thin dividers and line icons.

Its purpose is to turn the emotional Hero into practical B2B credibility.

### 9. Product section

The Products section is a hybrid editorial + catalogue composition:

- editorial text block at left
- compact product cards at right
- warm card surfaces
- real component photography
- minimal arrows
- almost no decorative UI

This is different from a conventional e-commerce grid.

### 10. Footer

The footer is a deep blue-green structural block.

It contains:

- ASH mark
- company identity
- small navigation
- contact
- copyright
- a very large, restrained line-art leaf motif entering from the edge

This closes the Nature × Industrial system.

## The central visual equation

**Paper + Structure + Nature + Metal + Editorial Type**

Or, more specifically:

> Paper carries the page.  
> Dark ink creates structure.  
> Leaf and water create movement.  
> Metal creates reality.  
> Typography creates hierarchy.

## What the previous prototype got wrong

The first HTML prototype was structurally useful, but visually too abstract.

It relied too much on:

- generic large typography
- a synthetic circular hero graphic
- a mostly single-typeface system
- insufficient photographic materiality
- insufficient natural-form integration
- insufficient editorial composition

The next visual pass should therefore prioritize **realistic visual assets and composition**, rather than adding more generic UI.
